Add a Session

Upload SwingSync CSV

Export your session from SwingSync as CSV and drop it in here. It's parsed in your browser and saved locally — nothing gets sent anywhere, and you don't need to come back and ask for a rebuild. If the file contains a small trailing block of wedge-only, short-carry shots, it's auto-detected and filed under Short Game rather than mixed into range data.

Log a Cue or Note

No need for a club or CSV here — just a quick note on what you were working on, so future-you can line it up against the trend charts. Saved locally alongside your sessions.

Reset Local Data

Clears any sessions or notes you've added on this device and reverts to the original uploaded dataset. Doesn't affect anything in SwingSync itself.
